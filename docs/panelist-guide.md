# Panelist Guide

Panelists join from their own device — phone, tablet, or laptop — without needing to share a screen.

## 1. Join the session

Open a browser and go to:

```
http://<hostname>:8000/join
```

The moderator will tell you the hostname (it's shown next to the session code on their screen).

Enter:
- **Your name** — this is how your score is attributed in the results
- **Session code** — the 6-character code shown on the moderator's screen (e.g. `C30G3V`)

Click **Join Session**.

## 2. Read the resume

When the moderator opens a resume, the PDF appears on your screen automatically. Use the **◀ Prev / Next ▶** buttons to page through it and **− / +** to zoom in or out.

If no resume is shown yet, a waiting message is displayed until the moderator opens one.

## 3. Submit your score

When the moderator opens voting, a star-rating widget appears on the right side of your screen.

Click a star to select your score (1–5). Your selection is submitted immediately — there is no separate confirm button. Once submitted, your score is locked in and a confirmation panel replaces the widget showing your chosen rating.

You can see how many others have submitted alongside your confirmation (e.g. `2 of 5 submitted`), but you cannot see anyone else's actual score until the moderator reveals results.

## 4. See the results

When the moderator clicks **Reveal Results**, the results panel on your screen updates to show all scores and the average across the panel.

The process then repeats for the next resume — the PDF on your screen updates automatically when the moderator opens the next one.
